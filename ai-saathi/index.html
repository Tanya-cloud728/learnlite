WHY AI SAATHI?

Technology that helps everyone

AI Saathi aims to make everyday information easier to understand. It can assist with images and text, but it may make mistakes. Always verify important instructions, safety warnings, and official information.

AI Saathi is an assistive prototype, not a replacement for a trained guide, emergency service, or professional advice.
